## Skincare 
I have very congested skin ever since I was a teenager, while using a recommended treatment of glycolic acid I developed after a day of long sun exposure (where I think I didn't reaply enough sunscreen) a melasma shadow on my upper lip. Since I'm nearing 40s I also try to add some anti aging to my routine. 
- Face wash: Cerave foaming face wash
- AM routine:
	- Niacinamide + Zinc from the The Ordinary
	- Mineral sunscreen from CeraVe
- PM routine:
	- Treatments: I really like the following products:
		- Salycilic acid serum - Anko (from Kmart AU)
		- Azelaic Acid booster - Paula's Choice
		- Vitamin C serum - Tessa (Chilean brand)
	- Hydration:
		- CeraVe Moisterizing Lotion
