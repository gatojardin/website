      • Less unnecessary shopping.
        • No new tech. I have everything I need at the moment
        • Only 1 new piece of clothing a month or a 3-piece outfit a season.
          • Have to finish my current sewing projects (2 Pomona pants) before beginning a new one.
        • No new stationery or art supply (again I have what I need).
        • Home and travel

🖥 Watching

  • Finished my Clarissa flashback.
  • Saw Kaos (before finding out it was cancelled). Really liked it. I would describe it as a sort of Greek mythology Romeo + Juliet. One of the most interesting adaptation details was turning the primordial and conceptual Greek gods, that absolutely can't comprehend human beings into unfeeling billionaires. It's not a correct interpretation, but it's very interesting modernization and works quite well in the narrative.
